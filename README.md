## Toygar Tanyel

I work on artificial intelligence, but what I am really curious about is how this
technology will coexist with people. In practice that has meant applied and interpretable
machine learning: making a model's decision legible to whoever has to act on it, across
medical imaging, biosignals and language models.

**→ [tanyelai.github.io](https://tanyelai.github.io)** — research, publications, notes

Founding AI engineer at [Promake](https://promake.ai). Visiting researcher at the
University of Southern Denmark, Centre for Industrial Software.

### Code behind the papers

- **[vcce](https://github.com/tanyelai/vcce)** — Interpretable ECG analysis for myocardial
  infarction detection through counterfactuals.
  [*Biomedical Signal Processing and Control*, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bspc.2024.107227)
- **[deep-breast-positioning](https://github.com/tanyelai/deep-breast-positioning)** —
  Mammographic breast positioning assessment via deep learning: landmarks, the posterior
  nipple line, and the labels to go with them.
  [*Deep-Breast workshop, MICCAI 2024*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10796)
- **[save-the-kid](https://github.com/tanyelai/save-the-kid)** — Deciphering
  machine-learning decisions to distinguish between posterior fossa tumour types using MRI
  features. [*Cancers*, 2023](https://doi.org/10.3390/cancers15164015)
- **[counterfactual-explanations-for-medical-research](https://github.com/tanyelai/counterfactual-explanations-for-medical-research)**
  — Beyond known reality: exploiting counterfactual explanations for medical research.
  [*arXiv:2307.02131*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.02131)
- **[lingda](https://github.com/tanyelai/lingda)** — Linguistic-based data augmentation for
  offensive language detection. *UBMK 2022*
